A minified version of a peerconnection channel sample from WebRTC samples.
Two peers are connected through a BroadcastChannel API.
It may not advance us right into a final goal of streaming tracks to network,
but it is a concise intro into creation and setup of RTCPeerConnection objects.
Launch the page in two windows; click the start buttons in both,
to stream contents of canvasses mutually from one window to another.
